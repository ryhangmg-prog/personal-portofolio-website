Why Work With Me

Most design problems are actually reading problems. Somebody hasn't said, clearly enough, what they're offering and who it's for — and everything downstream, the layout, the colour, the tone, is trying to compensate for that gap. I start there before I start anywhere else.

Good design doesn't decorate a message. It's the fastest way for the message to arrive.

If you already have words but no shape for them, I'll build the shape. If you have a shape but the words feel thin, I'll write them. And if you have neither yet, that's fine too. Most projects start as a rough idea and a deadline, and that's enough for us to begin.

What you get isn't a template filled with your logo. It's a small number of deliberate choices, made specifically for what you're trying to say, and nothing left in just because it's expected.
